About the Workshop

Recent advances such as DeepMind's AlphaFold and the LEAN project have demonstrated the transformative potential of AI in scientific research. Our workshop will explore how AI can advance economic and social science research. Our primary focus will be AI for economic research, but we may also address some of the economic and social impacts of AI.

Format

This is a workshop, not a conference — expect a mix of talks and collaborative working time.

  • Monday–Tuesday & Wednesday morning: Keynote and contributed talks with discussion time between sessions.
  • Wednesday afternoon: Submit questions and ideas, vote on favourites, form working groups. Then excursion and ceilidh.
  • Thursday: Small-group collaborative work all day on topics from the Wednesday session.
  • Friday: Group presentations (voluntary), audience vote, prize for the winner. Half day, packed lunch, ends ~12:45.

Lunches and coffee breaks are provided throughout. Evening meals are pay-your-own-way.

Venue & Travel

Bayes Centre, 47 Potterrow, Edinburgh EH8 9BT

ICMS is on Level 5. The building opens at 9 am. Pick up your access badge on arrival and return your swipe card on the last day.

Getting Around

Edinburgh's city centre is compact and most things are walkable from the venue. Taxis, Uber, and Bolt are all available.

ETA / Visas

From April 2025, visitors who do not need a visa for short UK stays and do not have UK immigration status must obtain an Electronic Travel Authorisation (ETA) before travelling. Check gov.uk for details.

Accommodation

ICMS has arranged accommodation for some participants — Ka Yin Au (Event Coordinator) has the details. Other participants have self-booked.

Excursion & Ceilidh

Wednesday Afternoon: Arthur's Seat

We'll walk up Arthur's Seat, an ancient volcano in central Edinburgh with spectacular views. The walk is straightforward but steep in places — wear shoes with good grip and bring a waterproof layer.

Bad weather plan: National Museum of Scotland, Chambers Street (free, 5 min walk from venue).

Self-guided routes: geowalks.scot

An alternative meeting point will be provided for those who want to skip the walk and head straight to dinner.

Wednesday Evening: Ceilidh

Stramash, Cowgate — 9:30 pm to 11:30 pm

Free entry, no booking required (but arrive promptly as it gets busy).

Plan dinner between 7–9 pm to leave time.

A ceilidh (KAY-lee) is a traditional Scottish social dance with live folk music. Dances are called out as you go — no experience needed!
