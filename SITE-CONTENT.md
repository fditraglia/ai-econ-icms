## Workshop Description (from ICMS page)
> Recent advances such as DeepMind's AlphaFold and the LEAN project have demonstrated the transformative potential of AI in scientific research. Our workshop will explore how AI can advance economic and social science research. Our primary focus will be AI for economic research, but we may also address some of the economic and social impacts of AI.

## Workshop Format
- **Not a conference** -- a workshop. Talks + collaborative working time.
- Mon--Tue + Wed morning: keynote + contributed talks with discussion time between.
- Wed afternoon: submit questions/ideas, vote on favourites, form working groups. Then excursion + ceilidh.
- Thu: small-group collaborative work all day on topics from Wed session.
- Fri: group presentations (voluntary), audience vote, prize for winner. Half day, packed lunch, ends ~12:45.
- Lunches and coffee breaks provided throughout.
- Evening meals are pay-your-own-way.

## Wednesday Excursion: Arthur's Seat
- Walk up Arthur's Seat, ancient volcano in central Edinburgh with spectacular views.
- Straightforward but steep in places. Wear good-grip shoes, bring waterproof layer.
- If weather is bad: National Museum of Scotland, Chambers Street (free, 5 min walk from venue).
- Self-guided routes: https://geowalks.scot/arthurs-seat/arthurs-seat-self-guided-walks/
- Alternative meeting point for those who want to skip the walk and head straight to dinner.

## Wednesday Evening: Ceilidh
- **Venue:** Stramash, Cowgate
- **Time:** 9:30 pm -- 11:30 pm
- Free, no booking required (but arrive promptly as it gets busy).
- Plan dinner 7--9 pm to leave time.
- A ceilidh (KAY-lee) = traditional Scottish social dance with live folk music. Dances called out as you go, no experience needed.
